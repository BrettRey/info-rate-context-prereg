## Where things stand

The components are built and tested: the counters with their forks, segmentation and onset inventories, the Stage 4 pipeline for a supplied document, both estimators with the fourteen and three rungs, the B/10 refit sensitivity driver, and the Stage 6 assembly (R<sub>k</sub>, contrasts, both intervals, fork shares, family weighting, the specification curve). The full suite passes on the merged branch with T13 (f32b234: 683 tests, two skipped for real data).

What's missing is everything that turns real corpus files into those components' inputs and their outputs into the report, plus the NS lens. The procedural rule (§4a: real-text entropy only "by one documented command" after the freeze) needs that command to exist and be frozen, and the freeze's file list must cover it.

## Decisions for Brett (as proposed; see Adopted above)

1. **Same text across options.** A line that fails under any option of its language is dropped from all of that language's options, counted and reported, in place of the current rule that blocks its whole document. Claude's view: this is §3's "same text across options" made operational.
2. **Context at a dropped line** under cross-line treatment: reset it, as split-at-gaps does (recommended), or join the lines either side.
3. **Mojibake.** Drop a subtitle file in which 10% or more of lines are mojibake, and drop mojibake lines elsewhere. On the scan's test that is two Thai files and about 20 stray lines (`DECISIONS.md`, 2026-10-11); P1 fixes the test itself as code before it runs, and reports what it catches.
8. **Counts before the stamp.** The counts phase (B, splits, dropped lines, onset inventories) computes syllable counts on the full corpora, never on the read texts, and no entropy. The tally exception was granted for the tally sample; this extends it to the corpora. Without it, B, and with it the cost and the local-or-Modal decision, would only be known after the stamp.
9. **The secondary run** (all 15 texts, digits and letters spelled out; decided 2026-10-08 and restated 2026-10-09 as running "both ways"). Its conventions must be fixed before any count meets NS, so before the freeze. Options: (a) keep it and write the conventions (P11); (b) drop it and report the primary subset only (7 to 15 texts per language; P1, P9, Q0 and Q1 in all 17), with the matched-count rate and the paper's formula recomputed on that subset, which §6 already provides. Claude's view: (b). The paper doesn't record how the readers read these tokens; many are ambiguous (a phone number digit by digit or in groups; *6.15* as six fifteen or a quarter past six); Finnish, Hungarian, Turkish and Basque inflect numerals in context; so the conventions would be a fork of their own that NS can't arbitrate, at the cost of 191 sourced readings. Under (a) the readings must also be written blind to NS (P11), which rules Claude out as the writer and leaves them partly unsourced, since a writer with no file or web access can't consult grammars. (a) is the choice if the fifteen-text comparison with the paper matters more than that.
4. **Word segmenters for CMN, YUE and VIE.** §2.2 lists two each (jieba or pkuseg; PyCantonese or cantoseg; underthesea or pyvi), and Thai's two are a fork already. They change only the word-aware rungs, but those include 1w, the baseline of the headline contrast. Options: (a) keep them as forks, as the spec says (recommended: the headline depends on them, and it matches Thai); (b) one main segmenter each with the second reported, as for the Japanese and Thai readers. (a) doubles the estimates for each of the three languages.
5. **The plateau criterion.** The approved plan says a rung that fails the plateau check is flagged as data-limited, not dropped, but never says what failing is. Proposal: report every language's curve at B/4, B/2 and B (and 3B where reached) at every rung, which adds a B/4 run to the approved B, B/2 and 3B (cheap, but new), and flag a rung when its cross-entropy falls by more than 0.05 bits per syllable from B/2 to B. The figure is of the order of the estimators' own error on fake data (KenLM recovers rungs 0–1b within 0.04 bits in F1c; the neural model's cost reaches 0.18 bits where its context can reach the dependency). The alternative is a relative rule (the B/2-to-B drop more than half the B/4-to-B/2 drop). Brett's call, made before any real curve exists.
6. **The 0 and 1w endpoints.** Already settled by working default item 2 (2026-10-08, open to Brett's veto): held-out cross-entropy at every rung, 0 and 1w included, with the plug-in estimates reported beside them at 0 and 1w for continuity with the paper (`SPEC.md`, Stage 5). The main runner follows it; the B/10 refit tool uses training plug-ins at 0 and 1w (T12's open question 7), so its refits measure a slightly different quantity from the headline. P5 brings the refit tool into line and has the runner emit the plug-ins beside the held-out values. Brett: confirm item 2, or veto it.
7. **The Thai alignment default** in T14: syllables placed by their spelling piece, with the line a reported failure when the pieces don't reproduce the reading (the side agent's suggestion, adopted on 2026-10-11 pending Brett).
