## Rows the cut doesn't cover

The tally measures how many syllables a line gets, so it can only judge readings it measured. Brett, 2026-10-10: unmeasured rows keep their defaults and are reported as not measured; boundary rows are measured on syllable entropy once the pipeline segments syllables.

- **Not measured, no alternative implemented:** 18 (ITA-A/B/C: exception list beyond riuscito), 25 (FIN-A: unenumerated heterosyllabic inventory), 28 (EUS-A/C: recovering tool glides), 30 (CMN-A: default erhua list), 31 (CMN-A: erhua word boundaries), 33 (CMN-A: traditional erhua suffix), 38 (All options: secondary digit/letter readings), 40 (FRA fallback: isolated letters), 41 (CAT-B: final posa in the passage). Proxy frequencies for some of these environments, from `src/stage3_tally_exposure.py` (`results/stage3/tally-exposure-2026-10-10.txt`): Italian prefix-like *ri-/re-/pre-/de-/pro-* before a vowel 0.7% of tokens (row 18; the proxy misses unprefixed hiatus such as *biologia*); 儿/兒 0.1% of Han characters in Mandarin and under 0.05% in Cantonese (rows 30–33); no line with a digit in any language (row 38, so number readings matter only for the read texts' secondary run).
- **Not measured, the tally's figure is an artefact:** 23 (TUR-B: chains of deleted ɰ). Its 0.21% falls on words with no deleted ğ (*saat*, *faaliyet*, *tabii*, Roman numerals).
- **Boundaries, not counts:** 45 (CAT-B: hosts of vowel-less i and other words), 46 (VIE-A: tone-placement standard), 48 (FRA-B: changed internal-schwa boundaries), 49 (Shared: cross-word ownership), 50 (FIN: complete consonant segmentation), 51 (IPA options: full syllable types and stress), 52 (KOR-A: isolated versus phrase G2P), 53 (TUR-A/B: final and preconsonantal ɰ). These move syllable boundaries, ownership or phones and leave counts alone, so they change syllable types and with them the entropy. Consonant placement (maximal onset) belongs here too and is fixed before Stage 5.

## Reading the tally

- **Forks:** rows 16 (Spanish B and *día*), 26 (Basque B's shared R0 slots), 12 (Catalan function words), 5 (identical-vowel fusion) and 17 (Italian phrase-final runs). Row 5's largest genuine effects are in Italian B (0.25%, mostly short words such as *a* and *e*, apparently identical vowels meeting across a word boundary) and Finnish D (0.18%, *liian*, *maailman*); its 1.4% under Finnish C′ falls on words with no identical vowels (*kilometriä*, *alkaen*) and is set aside.
- **Frozen with a bound:** row 13's alternative takes the rarest dictionary entry for each French spelling; it bounds the homograph choice rather than proposing one, so the row is frozen at the prevalence default and the bound reported (Brett, 2026-10-10).
- **Excluded:** row 7's alternative makes every tied pair a nucleus and row 6's is the old one-nucleus-per-vowel-run counter, both rejected in the note. The tally shows how far the count would move if either rule slipped back in.
- **Fixed defects and diagnostics:** row 8 (espeak-ng's language labels read as phones) would add 0.93% in French B and 0.63% in German (+1,225 of 194,756); row 14's undecoded Lexique symbols would remove a third of French A's syllables. Rows 29 and 37 run one branch of the tally, which counts every whitespace unit as a syllable; it would count a hyphenated pair of written syllables as one and a symbol as a syllable, so both are defects (Brett, 2026-10-10), and their identical −315 is one measurement.
- **Failed lines:** none in any language except Vietnamese, where the base counter rejects 19 of 13,762 lines (foreign and run-together words such as *Sénégal* and *Nhữnggiảithưởng*), and rows 29 and 37's alternative rejects 748.
- **Words read by another language's rules:** 1.35% of French tokens, 0.60% of German and 0.06% of Basque, nearly all English words read by English rules (`DECISIONS.md`, 2026-10-10). Not a reading in the table.
- **Negligible:** every other measured row is under 0.05%, and nine change nothing at all.
